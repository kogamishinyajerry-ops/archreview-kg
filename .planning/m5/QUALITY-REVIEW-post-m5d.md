Calibration report shows 5 bins, but **only 1 bin has any samples** (the 0.8-1.001 confidence bin, 32 samples, observed precision 0.9062, abs_deviation 0.62%). The other 4 bins have `sample_size: 0` and the scorer's `mean_abs_deviation` computation explicitly averages only the bins WITH samples (`bins_used_for_mad: 1`), yielding 0.62% MAD — easily inside the 4% MAD threshold for full 10 pts. The M5.G blueprint requires Beta-Binomial conjugate calibration across confidence bins; we have it for one bin only. Reliability-diagram coverage is 20% (1/5 bins). Score should be discounted to ~5/10 until at least 3 bins have ≥`min_samples_per_bin` (5) samples. **Override does not mutate JSON.** Recommended scorer patch: require `bins_used_for_mad >= 3` for full credit, else linear-scale.
