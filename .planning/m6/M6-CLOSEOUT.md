## What this milestone does not claim

- No human reviewer labels yet. All 22 reviewers in the KG are synthetic
  (`demo-reviewer-*`, `smoke-runner`). The `weighted_precision: 0.87` /
  `weighted_recall: 0.94` numbers are sound math against synthetic
  Beta-Binomial labels, **not** field-validated. This is disclosed in
  the new `quality_score.json::recognition_quality.detail.label_provenance`
  block, in `READINESS.md:40`, in the M6 demo video shot 7, and now here.
- 18 of 19 active real-public PDFs are Massachusetts municipal building
  filings (Cambridge, Medfield), with 1 Minnesota (Hopkins MN) active.
  Multi-state expansion is the M6.5.W3 priority.
- Rule engine over-detects on door-width and corridor-width. Per-instance
  reviewer labelling will replace the count-level recall measurement
  (M6.5.W2). Until then, recall on volume-dominant rules has a
  tautological ceiling of 1.0.
- The pilot is single-evaluator, local SQLite, no authentication.
  PostgreSQL + multi-user + audit-log export = M7+.

  recognition_quality.

  to 25+ active across ≥5 US states. Acceptance: provenance files exist
  for new states + `archkg kg ingest-suite` shows non-MA active count
  ≥ 7.

## Process notes worth keeping

1. **The judge's brutal honesty paid for itself three times.** Round 1
   caught a tautological query, a misleading badge, an over-long quickstart,
   and the synthetic-label transparency gap — none of which the internal
   scorer flagged. Round 2 caught my round-1 fix that silently swallowed
   a SQL error. Round 3 confirmed the remaining -1 is structural, not
   sloppy. **Do not skip independent audit on future milestones.**
2. **`subagent_type='general-purpose'` with the persona embedded in
   the prompt** is the working pattern when project-local agents under
   `.claude/agents/` aren't auto-registered.
3. **Add CI assertion against silent disclosure regression** —
   `test_recognition_quality_disclosure_probe_never_silently_fails`
   in `tests/test_quality_score.py`. Any future `detail` block that
   could silently degrade to a `status: probe_failed` string should
   get the same pattern.
4. **Demo video = renderer + ffmpeg pipeline, not live UI capture.**
   Reproducible from `render_shots.py` + `assemble.py`, no running
   server needed. Honesty bonus: every number on screen is read from
   the committed artifacts at render time.
