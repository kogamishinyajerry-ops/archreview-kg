# P75-01 Opening Provenance IFC Summary Plan

## Objective

Carry the `layout_3d` opening provenance consistency coverage into the optional
IFC export report and IFC preview Viewer data.

## Context

P74 made semantic, measurement, host-wall, and all-three opening provenance
coverage visible in `layout_3d_summary.md` and Viewer/Studio. The optional
`layout_ifc_export.v1` report still needed to show the same preview metadata so
reviewers could audit opening evidence while inspecting IFC export artifacts.

## Tasks

1. Read opening provenance consistency from `layout_3d.json` during
   `archkg ifc export-layout`.
2. Add `opening_provenance` to `layout_ifc_export.v1` JSON/Markdown reports and
   IFC Viewer data.
3. Add fake optional-dependency regression coverage for the exported metadata.

## Success Criteria

- `layout_ifc_export.json` reports semantic, measurement, host, and all-three
  opening provenance counts.
- Viewer/Studio treats the counts as preview-only IFC metadata.
- Missing IfcOpenShell still degrades cleanly and no default dependency is
  introduced.
- No rule-engine, `issues.json`, or `review_state.json` semantics change.

## Guardrails

- Do not infer opening provenance.
- Do not perform wall-void boolean subtraction.
- Do not describe `layout.ifc` as review-grade BIM.
- Do not make IfcOpenShell a required dependency.
